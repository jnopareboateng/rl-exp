# Approval audit notes (43 candidates)

**Checked.** For each of the 43 drugs with a first US approval in 2025 (27) or 2026 (16): the earliest approval by a listed regulator (FDA,
EMA/EC, MHRA, PMDA/MHLW, NMPA), found by web search (regulator pages, sponsor releases and secondary reports); year, confidence (35
medium, 8 high) and evidence links are in `approval_audit.json`, the reasoning on the lines below. Five had an earlier approval in Japan
or China and leave the cohort (delgocitinib, difamilast, ensitrelvir, taletrectinib adipate, tebipenem pivoxil hydrobromide). The other
38, with three cohort drugs outside the audit that were dated by hand (copper histidinate, daraxonrasib, milsaperidone), make the 41
drugs of Test-T.

**Not checked.** When each structure was first public (papers, patents, registries, databases). Approval date is not disclosure date, so
every line below says "structure disclosure date: not checked".

**What `exposure.json` shows** (PubChem compound record creation year, calendar-year resolution): 40 of the 41 cohort drugs have a PubChem
record, created 3 to 20 years before the approval year (median 10.5; at least 5 years for 36, at least 10 for 22). The one without is copper
histidinate (its reference is the free ligand, so no record is matched). Across all 43 audit candidates the record year precedes the
earliest approval year by a median of 10 years (range 0 to 20). The structures of most cohort drugs were therefore public years before
approval; Test-T is a temporal cohort, not a set of unseen molecules.
