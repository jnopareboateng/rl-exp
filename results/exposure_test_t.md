# Exposure terciles on Test-T (exploratory)

Delivered similarity of RL-full minus C3 on the 41 common Test-T drugs, by how public the structure already was. A positive difference means RL-full is higher. Wins, losses and ties count drugs; p is the two-sided sign-flip test within the tercile. Exploratory: no adjustment for multiplicity, and a tercile holds only 13 to 14 drugs, so a difference between terciles is not a test of one.
