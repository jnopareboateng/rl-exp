# Paired comparisons on Val

Differences are A minus B: a positive difference means the first arm of the pair is higher. Wins, losses and ties count drugs. p is the two-sided sign-flip test on whole groups of related drugs; the interval is a descriptive bootstrap, not a test. Only a pair marked confirmatory (the Val-selected RL arm against the Val-selected comparator on Test-T) is a registered test, and for it only the delivered similarity; every other number is exploratory, with no adjustment for multiplicity.
